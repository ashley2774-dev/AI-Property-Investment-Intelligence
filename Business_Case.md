# 🧠 Business Case: AI-Powered Property Investment Intelligence

---

## 🔄 Current Decision Workflow (How Investors Evaluate Deals Today)

For first-time property investors in South Africa, evaluating an investment property is a manual, multi-step process that relies heavily on fragmented data and individual judgment.

### Step 1: Property Discovery
- Investors browse property listing platforms (e.g., Property24, Private Property)
- Filter by price, location, and property type
- Shortlist properties based on affordability and perceived attractiveness

👉 **Limitation:** Selection is driven by surface-level filters, not investment potential

---

### Step 2: Rental Estimation
- Search rental platforms for similar properties in the same area
- Manually compare listings to estimate potential monthly rental income

👉 **Limitation:**  
- No standard methodology  
- Prone to over/underestimation  
- Time-intensive and inconsistent  

---

### Step 3: Cost Identification
- Estimate additional costs:
  - bond repayments  
  - levies / rates  
  - maintenance  
  - vacancy assumptions  

👉 **Limitation:**  
- Often incomplete or inaccurate  
- Hidden costs frequently overlooked  

---

### Step 4: Manual Financial Calculations
- Calculate:
  - rental yield  
  - ROI  
  - monthly cash flow  

Typically done using:
- spreadsheets  
- online calculators  
- rough mental estimates  

👉 **Limitation:**  
- No standardised framework  
- Results vary significantly between investors  

---

### Step 5: Area & Risk Assessment
- Informally assess:
  - neighbourhood quality  
  - demand and vacancy  
  - long-term appreciation potential  

👉 **Limitation:**  
- Largely subjective  
- Limited access to structured area-level data  

---

### Step 6: Decision Making
- Combine all insights (often mentally or in a spreadsheet)
- Decide whether the property is:
  - worth pursuing  
  - worth negotiating  
  - or should be discarded  

👉 **Limitation:**  
- High uncertainty  
- Strong reliance on intuition  
- Difficult to compare multiple properties objectively  

---

## ⚠️ Key Workflow Inefficiencies

- ⏱️ **Time per property:** 1–3 hours  
- 🔁 **Low scalability:** Limited number of deals evaluated  
- 📉 **Inconsistent analysis quality**  
- ❗ **High risk of missed opportunities and poor decisions**

---

## 📊 Success Metrics

To evaluate the effectiveness of the AI-powered system, success is measured across both **model performance** and **business impact**.

---

### 1. 🎯 ROI Prediction Accuracy

**Definition:**  
The ability of the model to accurately estimate a property's return on investment compared to actual or benchmark-calculated ROI.

**Why it matters:**  
ROI is a core decision metric. Inaccurate predictions can lead to poor investment decisions.

**How it is measured:**
- Mean Absolute Error (MAE) between predicted ROI and actual ROI  
- Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE) for sensitivity to large errors  
- Directional accuracy (correctly identifying profitable vs non-profitable deals)

**Target:**
- MAE within acceptable investment tolerance (e.g., ±2–5%)  
- High directional accuracy (>80%)

---

### 2. 🏆 % of Viable Deals Identified

**Definition:**  
The proportion of truly viable investment opportunities correctly identified by the system.

**Why it matters:**  
The primary goal is not just prediction — it is **surfacing the right deals**.

**How it is measured:**
- Precision: % of predicted “good deals” that are actually viable  
- Recall: % of all viable deals successfully identified  
- F1 Score: balance between precision and recall  

**Target:**
- High precision (to avoid false positives)  
- Strong recall (to avoid missing good opportunities)  

---

## 📈 Business Impact Metrics (Recommended Add-On)

To connect model performance to real-world value:

- ⏱️ **Reduction in analysis time per property**
- 📊 **Increase in number of deals evaluated per user**
- 💰 **Improvement in average deal quality (ROI uplift)**
- 📉 **Reduction in poor investment decisions**

---

## 🧠 Product Success Definition

A successful system is one that:

> Enables users to evaluate significantly more properties, in less time, with greater confidence — while consistently identifying high-quality investment opportunities.

By replacing manual workflows with a structured, data-driven system, this solution transforms property investment from:

> **an intuition-driven process → into a scalable decision intelligence system**
